### Drafting checklist for your attorney
- [ ] **Employment/LRA risk:** Re-confirm creator-vs-platform and creator-vs-brand
      relationship wording so it does not read as an employment contract.
- [ ] **Escrow classification:** Confirm with a financial-services attorney whether holding
      funds (even briefly) triggers FICA/FAIS registration, and fit the ToS accordingly.
- [ ] **CPA analysis:** Which users are "consumers" for CPA purposes; which sections apply;
      do s54/s16/s49 provisions need specific wording or are exclusions permissible.
- [ ] **Takedown process (ECT Act ss77–78):** Make sure the notifications/takedown
      procedure is described (benefit of safe harbour requires complying with the Act).
- [ ] Confirm the fee disclosure format satisfies CPA "price and places" requirements.
