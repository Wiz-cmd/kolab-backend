# Kolab Acceptable Use Policy — template skeleton

> **IMPORTANT:** Skeleton only; not legal advice. Have your attorney review.

## 1. Purpose
This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) sets out what users (brands, models, photographers,
creators) may and may not do on Kolab. It forms part of the [Terms of Service]. Breach can
lead to content removal, account suspension or termination, referral to law enforcement,
and (where lawful) freezing or adjusting pending payouts.

## 2. Prohibited content
You may not post or transmit:
- Illegal content (including content that promotes a criminal offence or violates the
  Films and Publications Act / online content regulations as in force).
- Sexually explicit content where it involves persons under 18, non-consensual material,
  or content prohibited by law. **Kolab is not an adult-content platform.**
- Content that defames, harasses, threatens, or incites hatred or discrimination against
  a person or group (protected grounds include race, gender, religion, disability, HIV
  status — see PEPUDA).
- Impersonation, misleading metadata, deepfakes presented as authentic, or content that
  misleads as to your identity or credentials.
- Malware, phishing content, or anything that could harm systems or other users.
- Spam, link-farming, fake engagement (buying/selling followers, bots), or content whose
  purpose is to redirect users off-platform to evade platform fees.
- Content that violates intellectual property rights of others. (See the notice/takedown
  process under the ECT Act in the Terms of Service.)

## 3. Platform manipulation & abuse
- No vote/engagement manipulation, sock-puppet accounts, or coordinated inauthentic
  behaviour.
- No scraping, automated account creation, or API abuse.
- No evasion of suspensions, bans, or payment/verification controls.
- Do not attempt to circumvent security or safety systems, or test them without written
  authorisation.

## 4. Payments & verification integrity
- Do not misrepresent deliverables, inflate metrics, or refuse legitimate refunds.
- Verification materials (ID, portfolio proof) must be genuine and your own.
- Money movement must follow the escrow flow; no off-platform side deals that try to evade
  platform fees while using Kolab's infrastructure.

## 5. Enforcement
- We may remove content, issue warnings, suspend, or terminate accounts.
- We escalate to law enforcement for credible criminal conduct.
- Where we apply **automated moderation** (auto-flagging/risk scoring), users may request
  human review (see Privacy Policy, automated decision-making).
- Complaints or appeals: `[appeals@kolab.co.za]`.

## 6. Reporting
Report violations: `[abuse@kolab.co.za]` (include URL/user). We will review and respond,
and we keep reporters' identities confidential where lawful.

## 7. Contact
`[legal@kolab.co.za]`.
